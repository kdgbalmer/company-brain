# Business profile

## The business in one sentence
Harbour Coaching helps mid-career professionals who feel stuck work out what they actually want next and make the move within six months.

## Business name and owner
- Business name: Harbour Coaching
- Owner name: Maya Lindqvist
- Location / time zone: Bristol, UK (Europe/London)
- Website: harbourcoaching.example
- Main social profiles: LinkedIn (main), Instagram (secondary)

## How the business works
Most clients find Maya on LinkedIn or through a past client. They book a free 30-minute fit call, then join either 1:1 coaching or the group programme. Sessions run on Zoom; between sessions clients do short exercises and send a weekly check-in.

## What makes it different
Maya spent twelve years in HR and has sat on the hiring side of hundreds of career moves. She's practical: every client leaves with a decision, a plan and real conversations booked, not just "clarity".

## Things to know
- She works Monday to Thursday. Fridays are for writing.
- She never promises a new job or a salary figure.

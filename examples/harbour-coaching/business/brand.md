## Visual style
Calm, lots of space, warm off-white backgrounds. Real photos of Maya, never stock handshakes.

## Visual do and don't
- Do: one short line of text per graphic, big and readable on a phone.
- Don't: gradients, emoji in graphics, more than two colours per graphic.

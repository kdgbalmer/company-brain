# Customers

## Ideal customer
35–50, in a stable professional job (marketing, finance, HR, project management, teaching). Good at their job, quietly bored or burnt out. Often a parent. Can afford coaching but feels guilty spending on themselves.

## What they're struggling with
- "I'm good at it, I just don't care any more."
- "I feel like I've left it too late."
- "Every option feels like starting from zero."
- The Sunday-evening dread.

## What they want instead
Work that feels like theirs, without torching their income or their CV.

## Words and phrases they use
stuck, treading water, golden handcuffs, "is this it?", pivot, "I don't want to waste my experience"

## Why they hesitate to buy
Money, time, and fear that coaching is fluffy.

## Where they spend time online
LinkedIn on weekday mornings; Instagram in the evenings.

## Customers who aren't a fit
Graduates looking for a first job; anyone wanting CV writing only.

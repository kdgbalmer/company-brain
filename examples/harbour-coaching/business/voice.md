# Voice

## How we sound
- Warm, plain-spoken and a bit dry. Like a friend who used to work in HR.
- Short sentences. One idea per post.
- Specific over inspirational: real situations, not quotes on sunsets.
- Never shouty, never "hustle".

## Words we use
stuck, next move, a real conversation, test it, small step

## Words we never use
journey, unlock, manifest, crush it, dream job, game-changer

## Samples of real writing

### Sample 1
Most people don't hate their job. They just stopped choosing it about six years ago.

### Sample 2
The question I ask every new client: "If nothing changed for three more years, how would you feel?" Most go quiet. That quiet is where we start.

### Sample 3
You don't need a five-year plan. You need one coffee with someone who does the job you're curious about.

## Corrections learned
- 2026-03-03: no exclamation marks in LinkedIn posts.

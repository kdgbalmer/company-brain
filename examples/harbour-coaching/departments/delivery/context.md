# Delivery context

## What this department does for this business
Runs 1:1 coaching and the Crossroads group so clients make a real decision and start acting on it.

## What it needs to know
- One file per client in `departments/delivery/clients/`. Private.
- Recap email within 24 hours of every session: three bullets and the agreed actions.
- Ask for a testimonial after session 5 (1:1) or call 5 (group).

## Workflows we use most
session-prep, session-follow-up, testimonial-request

## Custom workflows
- [group-programme-kickoff](workflows/group-programme-kickoff.md): everything that happens in the week before a Crossroads round starts.

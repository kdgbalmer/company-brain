# Example: Harbour Coaching

This is what a brain looks like after setup. Harbour Coaching is a **made-up** one-person career-coaching business. Every name, client and number here is invented.

Look around to see:

- [business/](business/profile.md): what the interview produces: profile, offers, customers, voice, brand, goals, tools and rules
- [departments/](departments/marketing/context.md): context and role files for the three active departments
- [a custom workflow](departments/delivery/workflows/group-programme-kickoff.md) the builder made for her group programme
- [outputs/](outputs/marketing/2026-03-03-linkedin-the-sunday-dread.md): two finished pieces of work
- [memory/](memory/decisions.md): decisions and a day's log

Your own brain puts these folders at the top level of your Company Brain folder, not inside `examples/`.

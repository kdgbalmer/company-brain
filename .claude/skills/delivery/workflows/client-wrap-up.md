# Client wrap-up

## Purpose

End the work with a client well: a final recap of what changed for them, a clear next step (staying on, a follow-on offer, or a friendly goodbye), a testimonial ask if the moment is right, and a closed-off client file.

## Use when

The work is ending: "Sophie's programme finishes next week, help me wrap up", "close out the Northside project", "last appointment with Mr Ellis today", "Jo isn't renewing, write the goodbye".

## Load first

- `departments/delivery/clients/<client-name>.md` (goals, full log, actions agreed)
- The offer they bought, and any follow-on offers, from `business/offers.md`
- `business/voice.md` (tone rules, samples)
- `business/tools.md` (inbox, payments connected?)
- `departments/delivery/context.md` (the owner's wrap-up steps, if saved)

## Steps

1. **Read the whole file.** Compare the goals they started with against where they are now, from the log. If the file is thin, ask the owner for the two or three biggest changes they've seen.
2. **Write the summary.** For the client: where they started, what changed, what they've built or achieved, and what to keep doing on their own. Use their words from the log. Only include results that are actually in the notes. Never round up or add numbers.
3. **Tie up loose ends.** List any open actions on either side, any files or access to hand over or remove, and anything owed (a final deliverable, a last invoice). Flag money items for the owner; don't act on them.
4. **Pick the next step.** Ask the owner which fits: offer a follow-on or renewal (only offers in `offers.md`, at the price written there), invite them back later, ask for a referral, or a simple thank-you and goodbye. One next step only.
5. **Draft the wrap-up message.** Warm and personal, in the owner's voice: thank them, the summary in a few lines, the one next step, and how to stay in touch.
6. **Testimonial moment.** If the work went well, offer to run `testimonial-request`. Keep the ask separate from the wrap-up message unless the owner wants them together.
7. **Close the file.** Add a final dated log entry, mark the client as finished in About with the end date, and note anything to check in on later (for example a three-month check-in date). If sales is active and there's a follow-on offer in play, add them back to `departments/sales/pipeline.md` with the next step.

## Check before handing over

- Every result in the summary is in the client's notes. Nothing invented or exaggerated.
- Any follow-on offer and price comes from `offers.md`.
- Open actions, handovers and money items are listed for the owner.
- The client file is closed with an end date.

## Save to

`outputs/delivery/YYYY-MM-DD-wrap-up-<client-name>.md` (summary, message, loose-ends list). Update `departments/delivery/clients/<client-name>.md`. Show the message in the chat.

## Needs a yes

Sending the wrap-up message, offering a renewal or follow-on at a price, sending a final invoice, removing the client's access to any shared tool or file, and deleting anything. Show exactly what will happen, to whom, and how much, and wait for a yes. Client notes are private and never leave this folder.

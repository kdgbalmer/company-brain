# Session prep

## Purpose

Give the owner a short brief before a session, meeting or appointment so they walk in knowing where the client is, what was agreed last time, and what this one needs to achieve.

## Use when

A client session, meeting or job is coming up: "prep me for my session with Chris", "what did I agree with the Patel account last time?", "get me ready for tomorrow's check-in", "who am I seeing today?".

## Load first

- `departments/delivery/clients/<client-name>.md` (goals, log, actions agreed, next date)
- The offer they bought, from `business/offers.md` (to see where they are in it)
- `business/tools.md` (calendar connected?)
- `departments/delivery/context.md`

## Steps

1. **Find the session.** If a calendar is connected and the owner asked about "today" or "tomorrow", read the events and match each to a client file. Otherwise ask which client.
2. **Read the client file.** If there's no file, say so and offer to create one (ask the owner for the basics). Don't fill gaps with guesses.
3. **Where they are.** Two or three lines: their main goal, how far into the offer they are (for example "session 5 of 12", "phase 2 of the project", "second visit"), and the headline from last time.
4. **Actions check.** List the actions agreed last time, on both sides, and which are done according to the file. Flag anything the owner promised and hasn't done yet, so they can sort it before the session.
5. **Suggest a focus.** One or two things this session should achieve, based on their goals and what's open. Add three to five questions to open with. If the owner has a session structure in `departments/delivery/context.md`, follow it.
6. **Watch-outs.** Anything from the log the owner should keep in mind (a worry the client raised, a deadline, something personal they mentioned and might want to be asked about). Only what's in the file.

## Check before handing over

- Fits on one screen.
- Everything comes from the client file or the offer. Nothing invented about the client.
- The owner's own open promises are flagged first.

## Save to

Show the brief in the chat. If the owner wants it kept, save it to `outputs/delivery/YYYY-MM-DD-prep-<client-name>.md`. The brief is private and stays in this folder.

## Needs a yes

Nothing, as long as it stays a brief for the owner. Sending anything to the client before the session (an agenda, a reminder, a calendar invite) needs a yes: show the text, who it goes to and when first.

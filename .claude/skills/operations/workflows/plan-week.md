# Plan the week

## Purpose

Lay out the owner's coming week so their real priorities get protected time, around the fixed commitments they already have, and with honest limits on how much fits.

## Use when

The owner asks: "plan my week", "block out time for the launch", "I've got too much on, help me fit it in", or on a Sunday or Monday when they ask what the week looks like.

## Load first

- `business/goals.md` (this quarter's goals)
- `business/profile.md` (time zone, working days)
- `business/tools.md` (is a calendar connected?)
- `business/rules.md` (house rules such as "no calls on Fridays")
- The latest priorities and weekly review in `outputs/hq/` and `memory/decisions.md`, if they exist
- `departments/operations/context.md` (working hours, preferred focus times)

## Steps

1. **See the fixed week.** If a calendar is connected, read next week's events. If not, ask the owner to list their fixed commitments (client sessions, calls, school run, appointments).
2. **Find the priorities.** Take the top priorities from the latest HQ plan and `goals.md`. Ask: "Anything else that has to happen this week?" Then pick no more than three priorities for the week and confirm them with the owner in one message.
3. **Check working hours.** Use the hours in `context.md`. If none are recorded, ask once (e.g. "What hours do you want to work, and when do you think best?") and save the answer.
4. **Block the time.** Give each priority one or more focus blocks of 60 to 120 minutes, in the owner's best thinking time. Add one admin block for email and invoices, and leave a buffer of about an hour a day for things that come up. Don't fill every hour.
5. **Be honest about fit.** If the priorities don't fit, say which one won't and offer two options: move it to next week, or drop something specific.
6. **Show the plan** in the chat as a simple day-by-day list: time, block name, what it's for.
7. **After a yes on the plan,** create the focus blocks as events on the owner's own calendar, if one is connected. If not, the plan in `outputs/` is the plan. Anything that would invite or notify another person (a client, a team member) is a separate step and needs its own yes.

## Check before handing over

- No more than three priorities, each traceable to HQ, `goals.md` or the owner's words.
- No block clashes with a fixed commitment; times are in the owner's time zone.
- Buffer time is left every day.
- Nothing scheduled outside their working hours or against a rule in `rules.md`.

## Save to

`outputs/operations/YYYY-MM-DD-week-plan.md`, containing the priorities and the day-by-day plan. Save new working-hour preferences to `departments/operations/context.md`.

## Needs a yes

Creating events on the owner's own calendar: one yes for the plan as shown covers those events. Any event that invites or notifies another person needs its own yes, showing who, when and the invite text. Moving or deleting existing events needs a yes for each change.

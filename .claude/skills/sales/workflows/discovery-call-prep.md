# Discovery call prep

## Purpose

Give the owner a one-page brief for an upcoming discovery, sales or scoping call: who the person is, what they probably need, which offer might fit, the questions to ask, and how to close the call with a clear next step.

## Use when

A call with a potential client is coming up: "prep me for my call with Priya at 2", "I've got a discovery call tomorrow, what should I ask?", "get me ready for the site visit on Thursday".

## Load first

- `business/offers.md` (the offers, who each is for, prices, objections and answers)
- `business/customers.md` (ideal customer, struggles, hesitations, who isn't a fit)
- `business/tools.md` (calendar, inbox or CRM connected?)
- `departments/sales/pipeline.md` or the connected CRM
- `departments/sales/context.md` (how the owner likes to run calls, if noted)

## Steps

1. **Find the call and the person.** If a calendar is connected, read the event for the time and any notes. Find the lead in the pipeline. If an inbox is connected, read what they've sent. If anything is missing, ask the owner for what they know: how the lead found them and what they asked about.
2. **Sum them up.** Three or four lines: who they are, what they said they want, where they came from, anything they've already asked. Mark anything you're guessing as a guess.
3. **Check the fit.** Compare them with `customers.md`. Note anything that suggests they're a great fit or not a fit, so the owner can decide on the call.
4. **Pick the likely offer.** Name the offer from `offers.md` that most likely fits and one fallback. Include its price and format exactly as written. If no offer fits, say so.
5. **Write the questions.** Six to eight open questions in plain words, in order: where they are now, what they've tried, what they want instead, why now, what's in the way, how they'll decide (and who else decides). Use the business's vocabulary.
6. **Prepare for objections.** List the two or three objections most likely for this person, with the answers from `offers.md`. If `offers.md` has no answer, leave a blank for the owner rather than inventing one.
7. **Plan the close.** One suggested next step for the end of the call (send a proposal by a date, book a second call, start date), and one line the owner can use to ask for it.

## Check before handing over

- Fits on one screen. The owner will read it two minutes before the call.
- Every price, offer detail and objection answer comes from the business files.
- Guesses are marked as guesses. Nothing is invented about the lead.
- The questions are open, not leading, and in the owner's words.

## Save to

`outputs/sales/YYYY-MM-DD-call-prep-<lead-name>.md`. Show the brief in the chat as well. Set the lead's stage in the pipeline to `call booked` if it isn't already.

## Needs a yes

Nothing, as long as it stays a brief. If the owner wants a pre-call email, a questionnaire or a calendar invite sent to the lead, show exactly what will go, to whom and when, and wait for a yes.

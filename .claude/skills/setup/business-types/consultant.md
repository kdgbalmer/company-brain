# Consultant

## Who this is

Someone who sells expertise to other businesses: strategy, operations, marketing, finance, HR, IT, sustainability, compliance and so on. Work is usually sold as a project or an ongoing engagement, agreed through a proposal, and often bought by a manager or director rather than the person who uses it.

## Switch on first

- `hq` (always)
- `sales`: discovery calls and proposals are where the money is won
- `delivery`: running engagements, check-ins, wrapping up
- `marketing`: usually LinkedIn and email, to stay visible
- `finance`: invoicing by project, milestone or month
- Add `offers` if they want to package their work into fixed offers.

## First workflows to try

1. sales: `call-to-proposal`, notes from a recent call turned into a proposal
2. sales: `discovery-call-prep`, preparing for an upcoming call with a prospective client
3. marketing: `social-post`, a LinkedIn post drawing on a real (anonymised) piece of work
4. delivery: `session-prep`, preparing a project check-in with a current client
5. hq: `today`, the three things that matter most today
6. hq: `weekly-review`, what got done this week and next week's three priorities

## Extra interview questions

Ask these one at a time (one question per message), after the goals topic in [stage 1](../stages/01-interview.md). They only cover what stage 1 doesn't already ask; skip any the owner has already answered.

| Question | Save to |
|---|---|
| "Who usually signs off on hiring you? A job title is enough." | `business/customers.md`, "Ideal customer" (as the buyer, next to the people you work with) |
| "Which past projects can I mention in posts and proposals? Say if the client can be named." | `business/profile.md`, "Things to know" (note which can be named) |
| "What are your payment terms? For example half up front, or 30 days after the invoice." | each offer's "Price and payment options" in `business/offers.md` |

## Words to use

Add these to "Things to know" in `business/profile.md` so every department uses them.

| Say | Instead of |
|---|---|
| clients | customers |
| projects, engagements | jobs, products |
| scope, scope of work | package |
| proposal | quote |
| fees | prices |
| check-ins, workshops, steering meetings | sessions |
| deliverables, recommendations | results (when describing what's handed over) |

Always check the owner's own words first.

## Watch out for

- Only name a client or project if the owner has said it can be named.
- Proposals involve money: drafting is free, but sending one needs an explicit yes.

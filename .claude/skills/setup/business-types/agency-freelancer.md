# Agency or freelancer

## Who this is

Someone (or a small team) who makes things for clients: design, branding, websites, video, photography, copywriting, social media management, ads, virtual assistance, bookkeeping. Work comes in as briefs and is sold as one-off projects or monthly retainers.

## Switch on first

- `hq` (always)
- `sales`: enquiries, quotes and proposals
- `delivery`: briefs, onboarding, revisions, sign-off
- `marketing`: showing off their work
- `operations`: keeping projects, deadlines and the inbox under control
- Add `finance` if they invoice per project or chase late payments, and `team` if they use subcontractors or staff.

## First workflows to try

1. sales: `lead-follow-up`, a reply to a recent enquiry
2. sales: `call-to-proposal`, a proposal from a brief or call notes
3. delivery: `client-onboarding`, the welcome email and checklist for a new client
4. marketing: `social-post`, a post showing a recent piece of work and how it helped the client
5. hq: `today`, the three things that matter most today
6. hq: `weekly-review`, what got done this week and next week's three priorities

## Extra interview questions

Ask these one at a time (one question per message), after the goals topic in [stage 1](../stages/01-interview.md). They only cover what stage 1 doesn't already ask; skip any the owner has already answered.

| Question | Save to |
|---|---|
| "What do you need from a client before you can start a project?" | `business/profile.md`, "How the business works" |
| "How many rounds of changes are included before you charge extra?" | each offer's "What's included" in `business/offers.md` |
| "Where can people see your work? A portfolio link, case studies, a showreel?" | `business/profile.md`, "Business name and owner" (next to the website) |
| "Does anyone else work on projects with you, like subcontractors or a team?" | `business/profile.md`, "Things to know" (and suggest switching on `team` in stage 2) |

## Words to use

Add these to "Things to know" in `business/profile.md` so every department uses them.

| Say | Instead of |
|---|---|
| clients | customers |
| projects, retainers | jobs, packages |
| briefs | requests, orders |
| deliverables | results |
| rounds of changes, revisions | edits, tweaks |
| sign-off | approval |
| proposal or quote (use whichever they use) | offer |

Always check the owner's own words first.

## Watch out for

- Only show or name client work the owner says is allowed to be shared.
- Scope creep is a common pain: keep the number of included revisions and the extra cost in `business/offers.md` so proposals and replies stay consistent.

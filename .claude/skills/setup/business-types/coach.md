# Coach

## Who this is

Someone who helps people change something in their life or work through one-to-one or group sessions: life, career, business, health, fitness, confidence, relationships, leadership coaches, mentors and trainers. Clients usually buy a programme or a package of sessions, often after a free discovery call.

## Switch on first

- `hq` (always)
- `marketing`: most coaches win clients through content and trust
- `sales`: enquiries and discovery calls
- `delivery`: sessions, follow-ups and testimonials
- Add `offers` if they're launching or reshaping a programme, and `finance` if they mention chasing payments.

## First workflows to try

1. marketing: `social-post`, a post for their main platform about their core programme
2. sales: `lead-follow-up`, a follow-up to someone who had a discovery call but hasn't booked
3. delivery: `session-follow-up`, session notes turned into a follow-up email for a client
4. delivery: `testimonial-request`, asking a happy client for a few words
5. hq: `today`, the three things that matter most today
6. hq: `weekly-review`, what got done this week and next week's three priorities

## Extra interview questions

Ask these one at a time (one question per message), after the goals topic in [stage 1](../stages/01-interview.md). They only cover what stage 1 doesn't already ask; skip any the owner has already answered.

| Question | Save to |
|---|---|
| "Do you mostly work one-to-one, in groups, or both?" | `business/profile.md`, "How the business works" |
| "Where do your sessions happen: online (on Zoom or similar), in person, or both?" | each offer's "Format and length" in `business/offers.md` |
| "Tell me about a client who did really well: where they started and where they ended up. No names needed." | `business/profile.md`, "Things to know", noted as a real example the brain may describe without names |
| "Is there anything you don't do, or refer on? For example therapy, medical or legal advice." | `business/profile.md`, "Things to know" |

## Words to use

Add these to "Things to know" in `business/profile.md` so every department uses them.

| Say | Instead of |
|---|---|
| clients | customers, users |
| sessions | meetings, appointments |
| programme, package | product, plan |
| discovery call | sales call |
| enquiries, people who've been in touch | leads, pipeline |
| results, changes, breakthroughs (in their own words) | outcomes, deliverables |

Always check the owner's own words first: if they say "members" or "coachees", use theirs.

## Watch out for

- Never invent client stories, results or testimonials. Only use ones the owner gives you.
- Session notes are private. They stay in `outputs/delivery/` and `departments/delivery/`, and never go in a post without the owner's say-so and the client's permission.

# Local service

## Who this is

A business that serves people in person in a local area: trades (plumbers, electricians, builders, gardeners), cleaners, hair and beauty, massage and therapies, personal trainers, dog walkers and groomers, tutors, photographers, repairs. Work comes in as enquiries and bookings, often by phone or message, and reviews and word of mouth matter a lot.

## Switch on first

- `hq` (always)
- `marketing`: local posts, offers and reviews
- `sales`: replying to enquiries and turning them into bookings
- `operations`: the calendar, the inbox and messages, the way jobs run
- Add `delivery` if they look after regulars or longer jobs, and `finance` for quotes, invoices and expenses.

## First workflows to try

1. marketing: `social-post`, a local post (Facebook or Instagram) about a service, a seasonal offer or a recent job
2. sales: `lead-follow-up`, a reply to an enquiry that turns it into a booking
3. delivery: `testimonial-request`, asking a happy customer for a review
4. operations: `plan-week`, laying out next week's bookings and gaps
5. hq: `today`, the three things that matter most today
6. hq: `weekly-review`, what got done this week and next week's three priorities

## Extra interview questions

Ask these one at a time (one question per message), after the goals topic in [stage 1](../stages/01-interview.md). They only cover what stage 1 doesn't already ask; skip any the owner has already answered.

| Question | Save to |
|---|---|
| "Which area do you cover? Towns, postcodes or how far you'll travel." | `business/profile.md`, "Business name and owner" (next to location) |
| "When are you busiest and quietest in the year?" | `business/goals.md`, "This year" |
| "Where do customers leave reviews? For example Google, Facebook or a trade website." | `business/profile.md`, "Main social profiles" |
| "Do you have any qualifications, insurance or memberships people should know about?" | `business/profile.md`, "What makes it different" |

## Words to use

Add these to "Things to know" in `business/profile.md` so every department uses them.

| Say | Instead of |
|---|---|
| customers (or clients, if that's what they say) | leads, accounts |
| enquiries | leads |
| bookings | pipeline, deals |
| jobs, appointments | projects, sessions, engagements |
| quotes | proposals |
| regulars, repeat customers | retained clients |
| reviews | testimonials (unless they say testimonials) |
| local area, the towns they cover | market, audience |

Always check the owner's own words first.

## Watch out for

- Keep posts local and specific: the area, the season, real jobs (with the customer's permission for photos).
- Many enquiries arrive by phone or message apps the brain can't read. Ask the owner to paste them in when they want a reply drafted.

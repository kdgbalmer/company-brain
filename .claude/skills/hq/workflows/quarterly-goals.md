# Quarterly goals

## Purpose

Set up to three goals for the next three months, each with a number and a date, plus a short list of what the business is not doing, and keep `business/goals.md` up to date.

## Use when

The owner says "set my goals", "what should I focus on this quarter?", "let's plan the next three months", "I want to change my goals", or `business/goals.md` is empty.

## Load first

- `business/goals.md` (the current goals, numbers and "not doing" list)
- `business/profile.md` and `business/offers.md` (what the business sells now)
- The most recent weekly reviews in `outputs/hq/` (if any)
- `memory/decisions.md`
- `departments/hq/context.md` (if it exists)

## Steps

1. **Look back first.** If there are current goals, go through each one with the owner: hit, missed, or still going. Ask for any result you don't have. Don't guess.
2. **Ask what they want.** One question: "Three months from now, what would make you say this quarter went well?" Let them answer in their own words.
3. **Turn it into goals.** Shape their answer into three goals at most. Each goal needs:
   - a number (clients signed, revenue, enquiries per week, email subscribers, hours worked)
   - a date (usually the end of the quarter)
   - a reason in one line (why this one, now)
   If they give you more than three, ask which ones matter least and move those to "not doing". If they can't give a number, ask for their best honest target and note it as a first guess.
4. **Check they're realistic.** Compare each target with where they are now (from the numbers table or by asking). If a target needs a big jump, say so in one line and ask if they want to keep it. It's their call.
5. **Build the "not doing" list.** Ask: "What are you deliberately not doing this quarter so these three get done?" Suggest two or three from what you've seen in the logs and offers (a new offer they keep mentioning, a platform they've been dabbling in). Let them choose.
6. **Update the numbers we watch.** Make sure each goal has a matching row in the numbers table with "Now" and "Target".
7. **Show the change.** Show the new "This quarter", "The numbers we watch" and "What we're not doing right now" sections side by side with the old ones. Ask: "Shall I save this to your goals file?"

## Check before handing over

- Three goals or fewer, each with a number, a date and a reason.
- Every "Now" number came from the owner or a connected tool. Nothing is made up.
- The "not doing" list has at least one real item.
- The goals fit the business's current offers, or the owner has agreed a new offer is part of the plan.

## Save to

After a yes, update `business/goals.md` in place (sections: This quarter, The numbers we watch, What we're not doing right now). Keep the old goals underneath a "Previous quarters" heading at the bottom rather than deleting them. Record the change in `memory/decisions.md` with today's date, using [log-decision](log-decision.md).

## Needs a yes

Changing `business/goals.md`: show the exact new wording first and wait for a yes. Nothing in this workflow sends or publishes.

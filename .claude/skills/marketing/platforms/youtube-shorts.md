# YouTube Shorts

Rules for a small service business channel. Platforms change often: anything marked "check current limits" should be checked in the app before relying on it.

## Formats

- **Short**: a vertical video, the main format here. Usually the owner talking to camera or showing the work.
- **Short linked to a longer video**: a Short can point to a related full-length video on the channel, which is a good way to move viewers to longer, deeper content.
- **Reusing videos from TikTok or Instagram Reels**: fine, but upload the original file without another app's watermark.

## Sizes

| Format | Size | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| Short | 1080 × 1920 px | 9:16 vertical |
| Thumbnail for the Short | Picked from a frame in the video, or uploaded in the app (check current limits) | 9:16 |

Keep text and faces in the middle. The title, channel name and buttons cover the bottom fifth and the right edge.

## Length

Shorts can be up to about three minutes (check current limits). For most service-business tips, 20 to 50 seconds works well. Shorts loop, so an ending that leads back into the start helps people watch twice.

## Script and hook

- **Open on the point in the first second or two.** A bold claim, a question in the customer's words, or the result shown first.
- **Say the hook and show it as on-screen text** at the same time.
- **One idea per Short.** Short spoken sentences.
- **Shot list**: give simple directions the owner can film on a phone.
- **End with one next step**: subscribe, watch the full video, or comment a question.

## Title and description

- **Title**: the most important text. Under about 60 characters so it isn't cut off, written the way the ideal customer would search ("How much does a garden redesign cost?"). The maximum is 100 characters (check current limits).
- **Description**: one or two lines with searchable words. Links in Shorts descriptions often don't click on phones (check current limits), so point people to the channel or a linked video instead.

## Hashtags

1 to 3 specific ones in the description or title. `#Shorts` is optional; YouTube recognises vertical videos on its own.

## Caption vs on-screen text

On-screen text and burned-in captions carry the message, because many people scroll with the sound off. The title is what search and the channel page show, so it needs to make sense on its own.

## What tends to work for service businesses

- Answering one common customer question per Short. These keep being found through search for months.
- Quick tips and "mistakes to avoid" from real work.
- Short clips from the owner's longer videos, talks or webinars (see the repurpose workflow).
- Before/after and process videos of real jobs, with the customer's permission.
- A steady habit: a few Shorts a week beats a burst then nothing.

## How posting works

- **Connected tool**: if `business/tools.md` shows YouTube or a scheduling tool as connected, the brain can upload or schedule after a yes.
- **Manual**: the brain gives the script, shot list, on-screen text, title, description and hashtags. The owner opens the YouTube app, taps + then "Create a Short" (or uploads a vertical video they filmed), pastes the title and description, and publishes or schedules. On a computer, YouTube Studio also uploads and schedules Shorts.

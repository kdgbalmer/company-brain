# Facebook

Rules for a small service business, posting from a business Page, and sometimes into local or interest Groups. Platforms change often: anything marked "check current limits" should be checked in the app before relying on it.

## Formats

- **Text post**: with or without an image. Good for updates, stories and questions.
- **Image post or multi-image post**: photos of real work, the team, the place.
- **Reel**: short vertical video. Reaches people who don't follow the Page yet. Reels can be shared from Instagram if the accounts are linked.
- **Stories**: vertical, disappear after 24 hours.
- **Events**: for workshops, open days and webinars, with a date and a link.
- **Group posts**: in local or interest Groups where the ideal customer spends time. Read each Group's rules first; many ban direct promotion.

## Text

- **First line is the hook.** Only the first few lines show before "See more". Lead with the most interesting or useful part.
- **Length**: short works best for most posts, 40 to 150 words. Longer is fine for a story the owner tells well.
- **Line breaks**: short paragraphs with blank lines between.
- **Tone**: warm and neighbourly. Facebook audiences are often local people and existing customers, so write like talking to someone you know.
- **Links**: links work and show a preview. Make sure the text gives a reason to click.
- **One next step**: call, message the Page, book via the link, comment, or share with a friend who needs it.

## Hashtags

0 to 2. They do very little on Facebook. Skip them unless there's a local or event tag people actually use.

## Caption vs on-screen text

For Reels, put the hook as on-screen text in the first second and add captions, because most people scroll with the sound off. The post text adds context and the next step.

## What tends to work for service businesses

- Local businesses: before/after photos of real jobs (with the customer's permission), seasonal reminders, "we're in your area this week", and asking for reviews.
- Coaches and consultants: useful tips, short stories, and answers to questions customers really ask.
- Questions that are easy to answer in the comments.
- Helpful answers in Groups, without a sales pitch, so people come to the Page on their own.
- Replying to every comment and message quickly. Facebook shows response times on the Page.

## How posting works

- **Connected tool**: if `business/tools.md` shows Facebook or a scheduling tool as connected, the brain can schedule Page posts after a yes. Group posts are almost always posted by hand.
- **Manual**: the brain gives the text and image. The owner opens their Page (on the Facebook app or Meta Business Suite), creates a post, pastes the text, adds the image and posts or schedules it.

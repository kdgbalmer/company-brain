# Instagram

Rules for a small service business account. Platforms change often: anything marked "check current limits" should be checked in the app before relying on it.

## Formats

- **Single image post**: a quote card, tip or announcement. Good for one simple point.
- **Carousel**: several swipeable images in one post. The best format for teaching (a tip per slide) and for before/after or step-by-step posts. Up to 20 slides (check current limits); 5 to 10 is usually plenty.
- **Reel**: short vertical video. Reaches the most people who don't follow you yet. 15 to 60 seconds works well for most service businesses.
- **Stories**: vertical, disappear after 24 hours. For behind the scenes, polls, questions and reminders to people who already follow.

## Sizes

| Format | Size | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| Feed image or carousel | 1080 × 1350 px | 4:5 portrait (takes up the most screen) |
| Square image | 1080 × 1080 px | 1:1 |
| Reel, Story | 1080 × 1920 px | 9:16 vertical |

The profile grid shows a cropped preview of each post, so keep the main words near the middle (check current limits on the grid crop). On Reels and Stories, keep text away from the top and bottom fifth and the right edge, where buttons and captions cover the video.

## Caption

- **First line is the hook.** Only the first line or two show before "more". It must make the ideal customer stop: a bold claim, a specific moment, or a question in their words.
- **Length**: anything from one line to a short story. Teaching posts and stories can run 100 to 300 words if every line earns its place. The maximum is around 2,200 characters (check current limits).
- **Line breaks**: short lines, a blank line between small groups of sentences. Easy to read on a phone.
- **Links**: links in captions don't click. Say "link in bio", or ask people to comment or DM a word to get it.
- **One next step** at the end: follow, save, comment a word, DM, or tap the link in bio.

## Hashtags

3 to 5 specific ones that describe the topic and, for local businesses, the place (for example a town name plus the service). Instagram has been cutting how many hashtags it allows, so check current limits. Put them at the end of the caption. Skip giant generic tags.

## Caption vs on-screen text

For Reels, the words on screen carry the hook in the first second, because many people watch with the sound off. The caption adds context and the next step; it shouldn't just repeat the video. On carousels, slide 1 is the hook and the caption backs it up.

## What tends to work for service businesses

- Carousels that teach one thing clearly ("3 signs your boiler needs a service", "What happens in a first coaching session").
- Reels of the owner talking to camera about a common mistake or question, filmed on a phone.
- Real behind the scenes: the workspace, a job in progress, prep for a client session (with permission).
- Real client results and testimonials, only with the client's permission.
- Replying to comments and DMs quickly. For local businesses, tag the location.

## How posting works

- **Connected tool**: if `business/tools.md` shows Instagram or a scheduling tool as connected, the brain can schedule the post after a yes.
- **Manual**: the brain gives the caption, hashtags and image or script. The owner opens Instagram, taps +, picks the image or video, pastes the caption and posts.

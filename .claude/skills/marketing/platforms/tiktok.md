# TikTok

Rules for a small service business account. Platforms change often: anything marked "check current limits" should be checked in the app before relying on it.

## Formats

- **Short video**: the main format. Usually the owner talking to camera, or showing the work, filmed on a phone.
- **Photo carousel (photo mode)**: swipeable images with music. Good for tips and step-by-steps, and a way in for owners who don't want to be on camera yet.
- **Stitch or duet**: reacting to someone else's video, for example answering a common myth in the owner's field.
- **Reply to a comment with a video**: answer a question someone left. Easy ideas, and it shows the account listens.

## Sizes

| Format | Size | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| Video | 1080 × 1920 px | 9:16 vertical |
| Photo mode | 1080 × 1920 px works best | 9:16 vertical (check current limits) |

Keep text and faces in the middle. The username, caption and buttons cover the bottom fifth and the right edge.

## Length

Videos can be several minutes long (check current limits), but 15 to 45 seconds suits most service-business tips. Go longer only when every second earns its place.

## Script and hook

- **The first two seconds decide everything.** Open on the point: a bold claim, "If you're a [ideal customer], stop doing this", or the end result shown first. No "Hi guys, welcome back".
- **Say the hook out loud and put it on screen** at the same time.
- **One idea per video.** Short sentences, as people speak.
- **Shot list**: give the owner simple directions they can film on a phone ("talk to camera", "show the before", "cut to the after").
- **End with one next step**: follow for more, comment a question, or "link in bio".

## Caption

Short: one or two lines that add context or a question. The caption limit is generous (check current limits), but people come for the video. Add searchable words the ideal customer would type, because people use TikTok as a search engine.

## Hashtags

3 to 5 specific ones that describe the topic and the audience, plus the place for local businesses. Skip generic tags like the "for you page" ones.

## Caption vs on-screen text

On-screen text carries the hook and the key points, because many people watch with the sound off. Add auto captions in the app. The written caption is backup, not the main message.

## What tends to work for service businesses

- Answering the questions customers ask most, one per video.
- "Mistakes to avoid" and myth-busting in the owner's field.
- Showing the work: a job from start to finish, a day in the life, the setup before a client session.
- Being a real person on camera. Polished and corporate tends to do worse than plain and honest.
- Posting regularly and replying to comments, often with a video reply.

## How posting works

- **Connected tool**: if `business/tools.md` shows TikTok or a scheduling tool as connected, the brain can schedule after a yes.
- **Manual**: the brain gives the script, shot list, on-screen text, caption and hashtags. The owner films in the TikTok app (or uploads a video they filmed), adds the on-screen text and captions, pastes the caption and posts. TikTok also lets you schedule posts from its website on a computer (check current limits).

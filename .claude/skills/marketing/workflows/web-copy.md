# Web copy

## Purpose

Write website, landing page or bio copy page by page, in the customer's own words, with one clear goal on each page, ready for the owner to paste into their website builder.

## Use when

The owner asks for words for their site or profiles: "rewrite my home page", "I need an about page", "write a landing page for my workshop", "fix my Instagram bio", "my website doesn't say what I do".

## Load first

- `business/customers.md` (above all, "Words and phrases they use", their struggles and why they hesitate)
- `business/offers.md` (the offers the pages sell)
- `business/profile.md` (what the business does, what makes it different, the website address)
- `business/voice.md`
- `business/goals.md` (which goal the site should serve)
- `departments/marketing/context.md`
- The current page, if there is one (read it from the website or ask the owner to paste it)

## Steps

1. **Pin the brief.** You need: which pages (or which bios), and the one goal for each page: book a call, send an enquiry, buy, or join the list. If it isn't clear, ask one question: "What's the one thing you want someone to do on this page?"
2. **Read what's there.** If there's an existing page, note in a few lines what's working and what's missing. Keep anything the owner clearly likes.
3. **Collect the customer's words.** Pull the exact phrases from `customers.md` for the problem, the result they want and their doubts. Headlines and section openers use these words, not industry terms.
4. **Write each page, top to bottom.** Label every section so the owner knows where it goes. Defaults:
   - **Home page**: headline (what you do, for whom, and the result, in their words); one supporting line; the main button; the problem as they'd describe it; how it works in three steps; the offers in brief; proof; a short "about"; answers to the main doubts; the main button again.
   - **About page**: why the owner does this work, told so the reader sees themselves in it; what makes the business different; a little personal detail if the voice samples allow; the main button.
   - **Offer or landing page**: who it's for and not for; the result; what's included; format, length and price; proof; questions and answers built from the objections in `offers.md`; the main button, more than once.
   - **Contact page**: what happens after they get in touch, how fast they'll hear back, and the one way to reach out.
   - **Bios**: a one-line version and a longer version per profile. Bio length limits change, so check current limits on each platform.
5. **One goal per page.** Every button and link on the page points to that one goal. Other links go in the menu or footer, not the body.
6. **Proof.** Use only real testimonials, results and client types from the business files. Where proof would help but there isn't any, put `[Testimonial needed: e.g. a client who ...]` and list what to ask for. Never write a testimonial.
7. **Search basics.** For each page, give a page title (around 60 characters) and a short description (around 150 characters) that says what the page offers and where, if the business is local.

## Check before handing over

- Read each headline as the ideal customer: would they know in five seconds what this is, whether it's for them, and what to do next?
- Is there exactly one goal per page, with every button pointing to it?
- Are the customer's own words used, not jargon?
- No invented testimonials, results, client names, numbers or credentials. Gaps are marked.
- Prices, links and details match `offers.md` and `profile.md`.

## Save to

`outputs/marketing/YYYY-MM-DD-web-copy-<page-or-site>.md`, with one section per page, each section label in order, and the page title and description at the end of each page. Show the first page in the chat and say where the rest are.

## Needs a yes

Publishing or changing anything on the live website or a live profile through a connected tool. Show the exact copy and which page or profile it replaces first. Copy the owner pastes in themselves needs no yes.
